Download the demo

A free demo for Windows, macOS and Linux. It plays the whole run, with fewer champions and relics to choose from. Version 0.5.0.

What the demo includes

  • 2 champions: the Ironbound Penitent and the Gallows Marksman.
  • The full run: three weeks on the map, events, shops, oaths and the Landlords.
  • The 4 starting relic families.
  • No Reliquary: the other champions, relic families and purse upgrades come with the full game.
  • Its own save, separate from the full game.

  • Windows

    Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. One .exe file, no installer. The file is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you: click More info, then Run anyway.

    Download (199.6 MB).exe

  • macOS

    One app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Unzip it and drag Dawnbringer to Applications.

    The app is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Right-click (or Control-click) the app and choose Open. If there is no Open button, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

    Download (149.7 MB).zip

  • Linux

    x86_64. Extract the archive and run DawnbringerDemo.x86_64. Needs graphics drivers with OpenGL 3.3.

    Download (113.3 MB).tar.gz
