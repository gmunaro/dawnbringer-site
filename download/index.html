Download the demo

A free demo for Windows, macOS and Linux, and on Android. It plays the whole run, with fewer choices when you build your hunter, and the rooms of the house stay closed. Version 0.8.0.

What the demo includes

  • Your own hunter, built before the run: sex, skin, eyes, hair, outfit and name.
  • A starting bonus, Riposte or First Blood, and a weapon, the Iron Club or the Old Crossbow.
  • Classes: Swordsman and Archer, then Crusader and Knight, or Hunter and Bard.
  • The full run: three weeks on the map, events, shops, vows and the Landlords.
  • The 4 starting item families.
  • The House with its 22 rooms shut: the other bonuses, weapons, classes and item families, and the bigger purses, come with the full game.
  • Its own save, separate from the full game.

  • Windows

    Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. One .zip file, no installer: extract it and run DawnbringerDemo.exe. The file is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you: click More info, then Run anyway.

    Download (360.2 MB).zip

  • macOS

    One app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Unzip it and drag Dawnbringer to Applications.

    The app is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Right-click (or Control-click) the app and choose Open. If there is no Open button, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

    Download (392.6 MB).zip

  • Linux

    x86_64. Extract the archive and run DawnbringerDemo.x86_64. Needs graphics drivers with OpenGL 3.3.

    Download (356.3 MB).tar.gz
