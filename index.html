Dawnbringer

A pixel-art roguelike auto-battler: build a champion, read each foe's gear, then pay the Landlord or fight.

  • Android alphaGoogle Play
  • Desktop demoWindows · macOS · Linux

Gameplay

What makes it different

  • Read the foe, not the dice

    Fights play out on their own and never roll dice. Every foe shows its gear, and the Stone of Despair darkens only before a fight that would kill you.

  • Build a champion

    Choose a second class, spend points in a skill tree and wear gear in every slot. Reheat items at the forge and set monster cards into them.

  • Your runs come back

    Fallen heroes rise and hunt you in later runs. Heroes who win can return on a Landlord's throne.

New in 0.5

  • Twelve second classes and a skill tree with three branches.
  • Gear by slot, reheated at the forge and set with monster cards.
  • A voice for every character.
  • Six biomes with their own plants, small animals and sound.
