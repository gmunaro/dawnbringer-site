Dawnbringer

The sun is dead. Carry its last light.

A roguelike auto-battler in dark-fantasy pixel art. Build a champion, read the exact outcome of every fight before it starts, and decide whether to pay the Landlord or fight it.

Gameplay

What is Dawnbringer?

Each run is three weeks in a dying land. Walk a fogged map of tiles, choose your fights, loot shrines and chests, and build a champion out of relics. Then the fights play out on their own.

  • Every fight is forecast. Exactly.

    Fights use no dice. The same build against the same foe always ends the same way, so the game tells you the result before you commit: “Victory with 29 HP left”. A loss is never bad luck. It is a build you can read and fix.

  • The Landlord comes to collect

    At the end of every week a Landlord comes for the rent. Pay the tithe from your purse and walk past, or fight it for its reward. Nineteen Landlords hold the seats of six lands.

  • The Landlord's night

    On the last night of the week the moon turns blood red and the Landlord walks toward you across the map. The clock shows how many steps are left. Spend them well.

  • Your dead walk again

    A champion who falls rises in later runs with what is left of its build. It wanders by day and hunts you at night, and beating it wins back part of the purse it lost.

  • Your winners take the throne

    A champion who wins is crowned. In a later run that legend may hold a Landlord's seat and fight you with the build that won.

The story

The Church sold the hours of daylight in deeds. Whoever held the deed to a land held its light, and the deed holders became the Landlords. They took more light than the sun had. On the Last Morning it fell.

A woman watched it fall and lost her eyes to it. She caught its last ember in her apron and lit a fire: the Last Camp. Mother Hesper lights a wick in each champion's chest and sends them east to carry the light to the Gate. A wick lit at her fire lasts three weeks.

Gold is sunlight gone cold, the only warmth left, so the Landlords collect. Every week the moon turns into a seal of red wax and the rent falls due. Those who fall keep walking, still carrying what they owe.

No one has crossed the Threshold with the light. What is behind it?

Features

  • 6 champions, each with a passive and a starting weapon.
  • 64 relics in 8 families. Family sets wake at 2, 4 and 6 pieces.
  • 19 Landlords across 6 lands and 3 weeks.
  • 77 foes: patrols, ambushes, packs, guardians and mimics.
  • Day and night on a map of regions. At night the shadows hunt you.
  • Dread: every kill makes the land harder, and makes foes pay more.
  • Stake your bank. Death takes the purse, never the bank.
  • 9 oaths to make the road crueler and the payout bigger.
  • 17 blessings, 8 consumables, 12 events, campfires, forges and wells.
  • Echoes, risen and crowned legends from your earlier runs.
  • Pixel art at 640x360, sharp at any size. Mouse, keyboard or touch.
  • 14 languages.
