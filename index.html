A dark-fantasy roguelike auto-battler with no dice. Each run lasts three weeks. Inspect every enemy's real gear before automatic fights, pay or fight the weekly boss, then walk home to bank your purse or risk it in a harder week. Death takes everything you carry.

Gameplay

Key art shows the planned v0.9 visual style. Version 0.8.0 uses vector characters and gear on pixel-art backdrops and UI, with partial controller support.

How a run works

  1. 1. Stake gold from your bank

    The sun broke, its glowing shards became money, and your House is your permanent base between runs. Before leaving, create a hunter, pick a starting bonus and weapon, and move gold from your safe bank into your purse, up to your purse limit. Purse gold buys gear, consumables, forge upgrades, and shrine blessings on the road, so carrying more makes you stronger early and raises what you lose on death. Death takes your purse, never your bank.

  2. 2. Explore three weeks and read every fight

    Walk a fogged tile map through days and dangerous nights. Every enemy carries visible gear and stats. Fights play out automatically with zero dice, so you inspect each foe and compare its build against yours before committing.

  3. 3. Pay the Landlord or fight, then cash out

    At the end of each week, the region's boss, called a Landlord, comes to collect taxes. In weeks 1 and 2 you can fight for the bounty or pay the tithe, which is 30% of your purse or a weekly minimum, whichever is higher. Week 3 has no tithe. After surviving a Landlord, walk home to bank your purse or carry everything into the next week.

  4. 4. Grow your hunter and unlock the House

    Learn a first class after week 1 and a second class after week 2, spend points on a single-map skill tree, assemble item family sets, and upgrade gear at the forge. Between runs, spend banked gold on 22 permanent House rooms.

What makes it different

  • Read the enemy, not the dice

    Combat runs on its own with zero luck. Every foe shows its real gear before you step in, and the Stone of Despair, your lethal-fight warning, darkens only if that battle would kill you.

  • Deep builds every run

    Pick your starting bonus and weapon, advance through two class tiers after each weekly boss, map out active skills, and combine 105 items across 10 set families with no-fail forge upgrades and monster cards.

  • Your past runs hunt you

    Hunters who die rise in future runs with their old gear, and one can even claim a Landlord's seat as a heretic. Join the optional weekly board and other players' verified hunters can wait in lairs as your rivals.

New in 0.8

  • Vector SVG art for hunters, monsters, Landlords, and equipped gear. Battle backdrops, map tiles, and UI still use pixel art in 0.8.0, combat effects mix vector shapes and pixel sprites, and v0.9 is planned to unify them in vector art.
  • Weekly challenge and online leaderboard. Everyone plays the same map layout, and a server replays runs to verify scores. You choose whether to share your city, your country only, or no place, which keeps your runs off public boards, and other players' hunters can appear in lairs as rivals.
  • Offline duels. Trade a hunter code with a friend or pit two saved hunters against each other on one device.
  • 8 active skills that recharge by rounds and 18 rule-changing nodes in the skill tree, plus slot-specific specialist shops.
  • Voiced hunter lines matching your chosen sex, a narrator called the Margin who comments on your run, and full Portuguese (Brazil) voice dubbing.
  • Accessibility upgrades including protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia palettes, shape marks on every color cue, on-device Android voice control, and live option diagrams.
  • A separate free Android demo app in Google Play closed testing.
