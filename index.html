Dawnbringer

A dark-fantasy roguelike where fights play out on their own: build a hunter, read each foe's gear, then pay the Landlord or fight. Try it free on your computer.

  • Join the Android alphaGoogle Play
  • Play the free demoWindows · macOS · Linux

Gameplay

What makes it different

  • Read the foe, not the dice

    Fights play out on their own and never roll dice. Every foe shows its gear, and the Stone of Despair darkens only before a fight that would kill you.

  • Build your hunter

    Pick your hunter's look, bonus and weapon, learn a class in two steps, spend points on a skill map and wear gear in every slot. Reheat items at the forge and set monster cards into them.

  • Your runs come back

    Fallen hunters rise and hunt you in later runs, and one of them can take a Landlord's seat as a heretic. Join the online board and another player's hunter can wait in a lair as your rival.

New in 0.8

  • Hunters, monsters, Landlords, gear and effects are drawn in vector art, sharp on any screen.
  • A weekly challenge: the same run for every hunter. Join the online board to rank it against players in your city, region or country (you choose what place to share), and another player's hunter can wait in a lair as your rival.
  • Duels: send a hero's code to a friend, or pick two saved heroes and fight on one device.
  • Your hunter speaks in a voice of the sex you pick, a narrator comments the run, and in Portuguese (Brazil) the characters speak Portuguese.
  • Accessibility: palettes for protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia, shape marks on color cues, voice control on Android, and a diagram that explains each option.
  • A free demo for Android, in its own app.
