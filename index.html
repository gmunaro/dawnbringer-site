Dawnbringer

The sun is dead. Carry its last ember.

Cross a dark land, build a champion from 64 relics, and pick your fights. Combat uses no dice: on the road, you see the exact outcome before you commit. When the Landlord comes for the rent at week's end, pay the tithe or fight for its head.

How it plays

A run lasts three weeks. You step across a fogged map, open chests, visit shrines and forges, and piece together a relic build before the week runs out. Fights play out automatically; the strategy is in the route you take and the build you put together.

  • Exact forecasts on the road

    Combat is 100% deterministic. Before you take any road fight, the game simulates the clash and shows the exact result, down to "Victory with 29 HP left". Only against the Landlord do you fight blind.

  • Pay the rent or fight

    At the end of each week, the region's Landlord comes to collect. Pay the tithe from your purse to walk away alive, or fight it for its bounty. Win, and you can either bank your gold and go home or risk the whole purse in the next week.

  • The last night's hunt

    On the final night of the week, the moon turns red and the Landlord steps onto the map to hunt you with every move. If your build is ready early, you don't have to wait: march to its throne and beat it for 50% more gold.

  • Your dead stay on the road

    When a champion dies, it lingers in that land on future runs with the gear it carried. It wanders by day and hunts you at night. Kill it to recover part of the lost purse.

  • Your winners become Landlords

    Champions who survive all three weeks are crowned. In later runs, they can take a region's throne and come to collect using the exact build that won.

The story

The Church parceled out the hours of daylight and sold them as deeds. The buyers became the Landlords, and they drained more light than the sun had to give. On the Last Morning, the sun fell.

Mother Hesper went blind watching it fall, caught its last ember in her apron, and lit the fire of the Last Camp. She plants a wick in each champion's chest and sends them east toward the Gate. A wick burns for three weeks.

Gold is cold sunlight, the only warmth left. Every week the moon seals the sky in red wax and the Landlords come for the rent. Those who die on the road keep walking until their debt is paid.

No one has carried the flame past the Gate. What waits on the other side?

Features

  • 6 playable champions, each with a unique starting weapon and passive.
  • 64 relics across 8 families, with set bonuses at 2, 4, and 6 pieces.
  • 19 Landlords across 6 regions and 3 weeks.
  • 77 enemies on the map, including patrols, ambushes, packs, guardians, and mimics.
  • Day and night cycle: at night your sight shrinks and shadows hunt you.
  • Dread: every kill makes the region deadlier and raises enemy gold drops.
  • Separate bank and purse: stake gold at the start and cash out after each Landlord.
  • 9 optional oaths that raise both the difficulty and the payout.
  • 17 blessings, 8 belt consumables, 12 road events, forges, campfires, and wells.
  • Past runs leave Echoes, risen champions, and crowned Landlords on your map.
  • Native 640x360 pixel art with mouse, keyboard, and touch controls.
  • Translated into 14 languages.
