Description

Dawnbringer is a roguelike auto-battler in dark-fantasy pixel art. The sun is dead, and every week a Landlord comes to collect. You walk a fogged map, choose fights, gather relics and blessings, and build a champion. Fights resolve on their own without dice, so the game shows the exact outcome before you commit. Before each run you stake gold from your bank; death takes the purse, never the bank. After each Landlord you cash out or carry everything into a harder week. Champions who die walk again in later runs, and champions who win are crowned and may return as Landlords.

Features

  • 6 champions, each with a passive and a starting weapon.
  • 64 relics in 8 families. Family sets wake at 2, 4 and 6 pieces.
  • 19 Landlords across 6 lands and 3 weeks.
  • 77 foes: patrols, ambushes, packs, guardians and mimics.
  • Day and night on a map of regions. At night the shadows hunt you.
  • Dread: every kill makes the land harder, and makes foes pay more.
  • Stake your bank. Death takes the purse, never the bank.
  • 9 oaths to make the road crueler and the payout bigger.
  • 17 blessings, 8 consumables, 12 events, campfires, forges and wells.
  • Echoes, risen and crowned legends from your earlier runs.
  • Pixel art at 640x360, sharp at any size. Mouse, keyboard or touch.
  • 14 languages.
