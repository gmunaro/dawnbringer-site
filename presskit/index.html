Description

Dawnbringer is a dark-fantasy roguelike auto-battler. The sun broke and the world froze, the shards that fell became money, and every week a Landlord comes to collect. Players cross a turn-based map, pick fights, and build a hunter: made before each run, then grown with a class in two steps, a skill tree with active skills, attribute points, and 105 items in 10 families worn by slot, reheated at the forge and set with monster cards. Combat resolves automatically without dice, and the game never shows how a fight will end: every foe carries visible items, so players judge each fight by reading its gear against their build, and a Stone of Despair only darkens when a fight would kill the hero. Against the Landlord it stays silent, and the player must pay the tithe or fight. Before each run players stake gold from their bank, and after each week they cash out the purse or risk it in a harder week. Between runs players spend the bank on the rooms of a house; each room brings someone who teaches a bonus or a class, makes a weapon, opens an item family or gives a bigger purse. Fallen hunters rise and hunt the player in later runs, and one of them can take a Landlord's seat as a heretic. Each week a challenge run is the same for every player, and an online board ranks it by city, region or country; players who join can meet another player's hunter as a rival in a lair, and two heroes can duel by code or on one device. Hunters, monsters, gear and effects are drawn in vector art. Every character speaks with a synthetic voice, a narrator comments the run, and the game is dubbed in Portuguese (Brazil). Options include palettes for color blindness, shape marks and voice control on Android.

Features

  • 6 first classes and 12 second classes.
  • Attribute points and a skill point every level; a skill tree that is one map, with 8 active skills that recharge in rounds.
  • 105 items in 10 families, worn by slot, with set bonuses at 2, 4 and 6 pieces.
  • Reheat gear up to +4 at the forge, and set 19 kinds of monster card into it.
  • 19 Landlords across 6 regions and 3 weeks.
  • Patrols, packs, ambushes, guardians and mimics on the map.
  • Day and night: at night your sight shrinks and shadows hunt you.
  • Your fallen hunters rise and hunt you; one of them can take a Landlord's seat as a heretic.
  • Separate bank and purse: stake gold at the start and cash out after each Landlord.
  • 9 optional vows that make the road crueler and the payout bigger.
  • A weekly challenge with an online board, rivals from other players, and duels with friends.
  • Every character speaks, a narrator comments the run, and each region has its own sound by day and night.
  • Palettes for color blindness, shape marks, voice control on Android and a diagram for every option.
  • A herbarium, a bestiary and 16 feats in the Journal.
  • Hunters, monsters, gear and effects drawn in vector art.
  • Mouse, keyboard, controller and touch, in full screen on phones, tablets and PCs.
  • Translated into 14 languages, and dubbed in Portuguese (Brazil).
