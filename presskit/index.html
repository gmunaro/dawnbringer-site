About Dawnbringer

Dawnbringer is a dark-fantasy roguelike auto-battler with zero randomness. The sun broke and the world froze, leaving glowing shards of sun metal as the only warmth and currency. Every week, a regional boss called a Landlord arrives to collect taxes.

Each run lasts three weeks across fog-covered regions. Fights resolve automatically with no dice, and the game never shows win odds. Every enemy carries real, visible gear you inspect before walking in. Your only warning is the Stone of Despair, a sun shard set under glass that turns dark only before a fight that would kill you, and stays silent against Landlords. At the end of weeks 1 and 2 you can fight for the bounty or pay the tithe, which is 30% of your purse or a weekly minimum, whichever is higher. Week 3 accepts no tithe.

Before leaving your House, you move safe banked gold into your purse to fund gear, upgrades, and services on the road. Death takes your purse, never your bank, and after each Landlord you decide whether to walk home and bank your gold or push into a harder week. Past runs also leave marks on the world: fallen hunters rise with their old builds to hunt you, a hunter lost in week 2 or 3 can claim a Landlord's seat as a heretic, and optional online boards let other players' hunters wait in lairs as rivals alongside offline code duels.

Features

  • Deterministic auto-combat with no dice. Inspect every enemy's real gear before stepping into a fight, with the Stone of Despair warning only when a battle would kill you and staying silent against Landlords.
  • Three-week runs across 6 regions of five zones each, facing 19 Landlords. In weeks 1 and 2, fight for the bounty or pay the tithe, which is 30% of your purse or a weekly minimum, whichever is higher. Week 3 has no tithe.
  • Separate bank and purse. Spend purse gold on gear, consumables, forge upgrades, and services on the road, then cash out after each Landlord or risk everything deeper. Between runs, banked gold unlocks 22 permanent House rooms.
  • Two-stage class progression taught by class master Abel Tarrow, with 6 first classes after week 1 and 12 second classes after week 2.
  • Attribute points and a skill point every level on a single zoomable skill map, with 8 active skills that recharge by rounds and 18 rule-changing nodes.
  • 105 items across 10 families worn in 1 weapon slot and 8 gear slots for 9 pieces at once, with set bonuses at 2, 4, and 6 pieces. Temper any item to level III and reheat weapons and armor up to +4 at the forge with zero chance of failure, and socket 19 monster cards. Charms can be tempered, not reheated.
  • 35 enemy types including patrols, packs, ambushers, guardians, and mimics, plus 31 fixed road events, 4 guilds with contracts, and 9 optional Vows, handicaps that add 10% victory gold each.
  • Fallen hunters rise and hunt you in later runs, a hunter lost in week 2 or 3 can claim a Landlord's seat as a heretic, and you can fight or kill 11 mortal NPCs.
  • A shared weekly challenge verified by server replay with an optional online board by city, region, or country, online lair rivals, and offline duels by code or on one device.
  • Vector SVG art for hunters, monsters, Landlords, and equipped gear in 0.8.0. Battle backdrops, map tiles, and UI still use pixel art, combat effects mix vector shapes and pixel sprites, and v0.9 is planned to unify them in vector art.
  • Synthetic voices for every character and monster plus a narrator called the Margin, in English and Portuguese (Brazil), alongside colorblind palettes, shape marks on every color cue, on-device Android voice control, adjustable text duration, and live option diagrams.
  • A 12-entry herbarium and bestiary, 24 rule-bending Oddities, and 15 in-game feats on PC, plus 1 Android-only postcard feat for 16 on Android.
  • Play with mouse, keyboard, touch, or partial controller support, with full controller support planned for 0.9. Interface and subtitles cover 14 languages.
