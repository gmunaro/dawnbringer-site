What's new

Every Dawnbringer update and what changed in it.

Dawnbringer 0.4.0

October 18, 2026 · Windows · macOS · Linux

  • No more exact forecasts: read each foe's items, and your Stone of Despair darkens over a fight that would kill you
  • 19 iconic monsters, each with the item behind its trick
  • 10 NPCs with quests, their own music, and dialogue that changes as you meet them
  • Carrion and Veil families, 32 new items, 19 new events
  • A short tutorial you can skip
  • Every hero gets a name, and you can change it
  • Sharper art on phones and big screens
  • A bug button on every screen

Dawnbringer 0.3.0

October 17, 2026 · Windows · macOS · Linux

  • Two new item families: Carrion grows after every fight won, Veil gives your hero Fade
  • 32 new items: shops offer more gear and more builds
  • 19 new events and two new foe lines, so runs repeat less
  • A bug button on every screen: capture, mark and send a report
  • A demo for PC on the website

Dawnbringer 0.2.0

October 16, 2026 · Android

  • The story: Mother Hesper at the Last Camp, voiced Landlords and champions, epitaphs and Fragments
  • The Landlord night: blood moon, a new score, and the Landlord kills on its way
  • Retuned difficulty; legends only from week 2
  • UI: Cyrillic/CJK fonts, tooltips, forecast colours
